## 1. IRS OPR Alert 2026-19 (READ, official IRS PDF)

- **Title:** Introductory Guidelines for Responsible AI Use in Federal Tax Practice. Issue 2026-19, June 24, 2026. Office of Professional Responsibility.
- **Official PDF:** https://www.irs.gov/pub/opr-taxpros/issue-number-2026-19-introductory-guidelines-for-responsible-ai-use-in-federal-tax-practice.pdf
- **Also:** IRS govdelivery bulletin https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USIRS/bulletins/41d6e70?reqfrom=share ; alerts list https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/guidance-and-resources
- **Nature:** guidance applying existing Circular 230 duties to AI. Not a new rule.

**Sections cited:** Circular 230 §§ 10.22 (due diligence), 10.27(a) (fees), 10.35 (competence), 10.36 (procedures to ensure compliance), 10.37 (written advice), 10.51(a)(15) (unauthorized disclosure of return information); IRC §§ 6713 and 7216(a); Treas. Reg. § 301.7216-1(b)(3).

**Key wording (verbatim from the PDF)**

| Topic | Wording | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Firm procedures (§ 10.36) | "Firms must deploy internal policies and procedures for compliance with Circular 230 in the AI space" | must |
| Staff training | "Comprehensive training on use of AI (the risks – technological and other – and the requirements)." Best-practice list: "Provide necessary staff training." | listed under "must deploy" |
| Internal rules | "Established protocols for secure data handling, AI accuracy monitoring, etc." | listed under "must deploy" |
| Vendors | "Outsourced or third-party AI tools should be vetted." Best practice: "Vet third parties' AI offerings when or before purchasing." | should |
| Documentation | "And all steps and processes documented to show adherence to section 10.36." Best practice: "Document AI usage and verification processes." | listed under "must deploy" |
| Taxpayer data | "Practitioners must strictly handle all client data using only secure, enterprise-approved AI." Best practice: "Never upload sensitive data to unsecured sites." | must |
| Client disclosure | Practitioners "should not only disclose, in general or specific terms as needed, the AI activities performed" | should |
| Review of output | "Practitioners must thoroughly review all AI-created documents and language"; "Treat the written text that AI generates as drafts." | must |
| Incidents | Best practice: "Prepare clear procedures for handling breaches or errors." | best practice |
| Discipline | Willful mishandling of taxpayer information through AI "may lead to disciplinary actions under Circular 230." | |

**What the alert does not say:** it does not use the words "written AI policy". "Deploy internal policies and procedures" plus "documented" is the closest wording. Describe it that way in the Word document.

**Use in the licence programme:** supports the training requirement, the evidence register (documentation "to show adherence"), enterprise-approved tools only, and human review. No source requires a knowledge test.
